The account and sync rows of `web/CLAUDE.md`'s navigation table, moved here verbatim in #2124 so a stand, CSS or band-sheet task does not load them. Paths in the "Start here" column are relative to `web/`, as in that table; `web/CLAUDE.md` keeps a pointer row for each surface.

| Retired text charts (#1740) | `../core/songbook/document-v2.ts` (`isRetiredSchema`), `lib/documents.ts`, `lib/repository.ts`, `lib/sync/repository.ts`, `lib/sync/drain.ts`, `checks/old-format.spec.ts`, `checks/account-retired-text-chart.chromium.spec.ts` | One chart schema (2). A text chart arriving as a file or a `#chart=` link is refused in plain words (`OLD_FORMAT_REFUSAL`); the server refuses a schema-1 Save body (`decodeSaveRequest`). One still SITTING in a store must never crash the app or stop sync, so it is stepped over where it used to decode, never thrown on: the guest and account `list` leave it out (the cursor still moves past it, and `outboxPage` bounds its merge by both pages' cursors), the home counts it unreadable, `remoteCandidates` and `drafts` leave out a text-chart candidate or draft, and `reconcile` replaces a text-chart song row with the account's newer version only when the account confirmed the row (a remote revision) and nothing is queued for it; otherwise it writes nothing (`'superseded'`). A sign-out or account deletion names such rows and writes them out as they are (`unreadableSongs`) before `clearAccount` removes them. The general rule for any row that will not decode, a text chart included, is the next row |
| A sync row that will not decode (#1769) | `lib/sync/repository.ts` (`prepare`, `newerSchema`, `acknowledge`, `removeUnreadable`), `lib/sync/collections.ts`, `lib/sync/repository-internal.ts`, `lib/sync/download.ts`, `tests/browser/account-unreadable-row.browser.test.ts` | One stored row this build cannot read must never stop sync for good. A mirror row (song or collection) that won't decode is stepped over and named with its remote revision (`unreadable` on the page); the outbox still VISITS it, and All songs and the home say how many songs couldn't be read — the home the larger of its own window's count and the full read's once one is held (`libraryUnreadable`, #1782), so it never says less than All songs — and both offer "Export as stored" (`useSongDeletes`'s `exportUnreadableSongs`, `app/use-song-deletes.ts`; the sign-out step's `(unreadable).json` files). Both also offer **Remove…** (#1805): `useSongDeletes`'s `requestRemoveUnreadable` reads fresh what the removal would do (`unreadableRemoval`, read-only, the same `removalHeld` check), `RemoveUnreadableDialog` (`app/account/purge-songs.tsx`) counts only the songs that will go, says first which are kept and why, says plainly the music can't be recovered unless exported and offers that export first (every song kept: no confirm, only the reason), and `removeUnreadable` (repository and loop, the loop's one serialization slot as `purgeLocal` takes it) removes, in one transaction and LOCALLY, each id still unreadable with nothing holding it — confirmed or not, since with nothing queued a confirmed row holds nothing the account lacks, and a cloud delete would destroy a version a newer build can read. An id is held (`waiting`, untouched — never one op out of a queue) by any operation queued (the step-over below, a newer build's queue, one no pass has visited yet) or by a draft that may be the only copy of its music (`draftHoldsRemoval`: a newer schema, or one this build reads; a text-chart or corrupt draft goes with the row), and the songbook's status line says what was kept and why (`removeUnreadableOutcome`). **A queued Save that won't decode** (`prepare`, one transaction): a queue holding a NEWER schema is stepped over as a refused head is, never retired (a stale tab may share the database); otherwise the WHOLE queue retires (receipts kept, never one op out of a `base` chain) and the record decides — a record that reads holds the newest Save, so one fresh Save of it is queued on its own remote revision and sent (`save()`'s refused-head precedent: nothing lost); one that doesn't falls back on the queue (#1782, `newestReadable`): when the queue's NEWEST Save still reads, it is re-queued the same way, on the record's confirmed revision, and `acknowledge` labels the record's bytes; otherwise (the newest Save is unreadable too, so an older readable one would roll the song back) the record loses its remote revision, so no download can take its unsent music for a mirror, and it stays for `unreadableSongs` to export and `purgeLocal` to remove. **A conflict whose account version this build cannot read** (`reply`'s `'unreadable-remote'`, #1782 — the lost acknowledgement above, or a newer-schema chart from another device) is stepped over as a newer build's queue is: `acknowledge` writes nothing and answers `'refused'` (the outbox's `refused` count), the Save stays queued and frozen, the record as it is, and the pass goes on to the next document. No conflict is parked (Keep both could not adopt that version) and never `remote: null` (Keep both's `'gone'` would delete the row). **The bump rule (DECISION 2026-10-18, #1782):** any chart or collection codec change an older build would reject bumps `schemaVersion` (no separate codec stamp) — `newerSchema` is the only way an older tab sharing this database tells a newer queue from a corrupt one, and without the bump it retires that queue; see the chart contract's Compatibility section. **The download** never treats such a row as a mirror (`planLibraryDownload`): a confirmed one is fetched and `reconcile`/`reconcileCollection` replace it with the account's copy when nothing holds it (no queued Save, no live draft, not on the stand; else `'superseded'`, nothing written); an unconfirmed one is not asked for at all; an `unsupported` body or a tombstone is stored as a candidate (a tombstone flags, never removes), which settles that revision for the next plan. A readable queue over an unreadable record still sends: `acknowledge` labels the stored bytes with the confirmed revision rather than throwing. A retired-format BODY from the server is `unreadable` and settled, not a failure |
| Account-local storage | `lib/sync/database.ts`, `lib/sync/repository.ts`, `lib/sync/collections.ts`, `lib/sync/repository-internal.ts`, `lib/sync/records.ts` | Native IDB transactions, owner/generation fence |
| Collections (#1474), on the All songs page with Starred as one (#1477) | `lib/collections.ts`, `lib/sync/collections.ts` (through `AccountSongbook`'s delegates), `lib/repository.ts`, `app/use-collections.ts`, `app/use-collection-actions.ts`, `app/collection-dialogs.tsx` | A chart carries no `kind`, so every chart path is unchanged; only the paths that move ANY document dispatch on it. Account collections are a store in the account database (version 2) because their Saves share the outbox's transactions; guest collections are a database of their own (`ensemble-v2-preview-collections`) so the guest songbook is never reopened at a new version. Songs are filtered when a collection is READ (`resolvedSongIds`), never rewritten out of it — deleting a song never touches a collection. **Starred is the built-in collection at ONE fixed id**, created LAZILY by the first star (never at startup, so an account at the document cap is never handed a doomed Save; a create at the local cap is refused with `CollectionCapError`), with no rename or delete affordance. Every edit is one read-modify-write transaction (`editCollection`), so quick toggles never lose a compare-and-swap. **A conflicted collection Save never asks**: after every outbox sweep the loop calls `mergeCollectionConflicts`, which retires the id's whole queue and re-queues the union (local order first, then remote-only ids) ON the remote revision — or, when the account has no such document (`remote: null`), moves the list to a fresh id. A song conflict is untouched (Keep both is still its only exit). Guest collections are adopted on sign-in with the whole-songbook offer (`adoptGuestCollections`): song ids remapped to the adopted ids, Starred merged into the account's, others under deterministic ids so a rerun queues nothing. Deleting a collection never deletes songs; its confirm's UNCHECKED "also delete the N songs in no other collection" (Starred counts as one) deletes through the existing paths, songs FIRST then the collection. The outbox walk catches a failed collections page (`outboxPage`), so one bad collection row cannot stop song uploads. **A collection moving never bumps `libraryVersion`** (which re-reads and re-validates every song): the loop publishes `collectionsVersion` for collection commits, merges and downloads, and `useCollections` re-reads on that alone. **Starred is guarded in storage**, not just the UI: both repositories refuse deleting, renaming or un-marking a built-in and `prepareDelete` refuses it (`BuiltInCollectionError`), because a tombstone on its fixed id would split Starred across devices; a `'gone'` Starred merges into another Starred this device holds — never into one this same merge is also resolving (that waits a sweep, so nothing ever chains onto a retired Save). A delete with "also delete" re-decides the songs at CONFIRM time from a fresh read (`fresh`), which may only NARROW the list the dialog showed, and asks the collection's own delete first (`collectionDeleteRefusal`), so a still-uploading collection stops it before any song goes |
| Whole iReal playlist import (#1478) | `lib/playlist-import.ts`, `app/import-dialog.tsx`, `lib/repository.ts` (`importSongs`), `lib/sync/collections.ts` (`importPlaylist`), `lib/account/sync-helpers.ts` (`libraryCheck`) | The summary is decided BEFORE anything is written: duplicates by title + composer (skipped by default, kept in the collection as the songbook's own copy, each named with what it matched), refused songs listed and never half-imported, the account cap stated and enforced. **An account import waits for a SETTLED library** (`libraryCheck`, `importRoom`, `LibraryFacts`; review R1, C1): a device with charts still to fetch for a transient reason is refused before writing; charts settled without a copy here (held, needing an update, missing, malformed) only earn a "couldn't be checked for duplicates" note. Room is `heldByAccount` over the manifest's charts and collections — never this device's rows alone — and the dialog starts a pass so it is checked against one made now. Reading and checking an export can be cancelled (Cancel, Escape); only the write holds the dialog open. The account write is ONE transaction — songs then collection, cap re-checked inside — so there is nothing to resume; the drain is the ordinary outbox. Guest songs and collection live in two databases, so two transactions, songs first; a rerun of an interrupted guest import finds the songs as duplicates and still makes the collection. Each song keeps its own link as `importSource`, never the playlist. Parse and per-song checks run in slices so a 1,460-song playlist never freezes the page |
| Account chart drafts and Continue (#1299) | `lib/writer.ts`, `lib/sync/repository.ts` (`liveDraft`), `lib/account/sync-loop.ts`, `app/ensemble.tsx` (`draft`/`open`) | A draft row counts for anything — holding a record against a remote body, a sign-out warning, a `retained` delete, an offer to the musician — only while it is LIVE: captured at or after the committed version it sits on (`liveDraft`, the rule guest `recoveryFor` already used). A Save retires every writer's superseded rows; a chart that is back at its committed version retains nothing at all, all writers, or the next open recovers the edit that was just reverted away. An account chart's unsaved experiment and its last-opened pointer live in the ACCOUNT database, never the guest `localStorage` namespace. One writer id per page load, shared by both stores. | Keep both after a refused Save (#1267); a bare tombstoned candidate (#1362) | `lib/sync/repository.ts` (`keepBoth`, `keepMineAsNewSong`), `lib/account/sync-loop.ts` (`keepBoth`), `app/account/conflict.tsx` | The only way out of a conflicted outbox head, which is otherwise terminal. ONE transaction: the local line is created under a fresh document id and a fresh operation id, every queued Save for the failed id retires, drafts follow, and the original id either adopts the preserved remote version or leaves the library (`gone`). Never reuses the refused ids; no merge and no "overwrite theirs". A `deleted` candidate outranks the refusal's own remote version; an `unsupported` one survives it. The shell commits the bar editor FIRST (both editors are keyed on `current.id`), then re-points the chart on the stand — id, revision and the marked `— kept` title — without changing a bar of the music. **`keepBoth` also resolves a bare `'deleted'` candidate with an EMPTY queue (#1362)** — the account tombstoned an id while a live draft or the merely-open chart held it, with nothing ever queued to refuse — through the same fresh-identity route (`keepMineAsNewSong`, the mint-and-move-drafts half both routes share), sourced from the newest LIVE draft (`liveDraft`'s rule, never just the newest by clock — a stale row would silently carry OLDER music forward) or the saved record when no draft is live; with no saved record at all every draft counts as live, and `'none'` only when there is truly nothing to keep |
| Deleting songs no longer in your account (#1754) | `purgeLocal` in `lib/sync/repository.ts` and `lib/account/sync-loop.ts`, `app/account/purge-songs.tsx`, `app/use-song-deletes.ts` | The way out of a song a cloud delete cannot remove: a `deleted` candidate ("No longer in your account" — `commitDeleted` kept it for a live draft or a queued Save, so another delete only answers `retained` again) or one the cloud never confirmed (`remoteRevision === null`). "Delete from this device too? Unsaved changes to it will be lost." — a marked row's Delete asks that alone (a never-confirmed one asks "Delete this song from this device? It isn't in your account, so this removes the only copy.", #1756; every single-song step offers Export first), and a cloud delete that answers `retained` goes straight on to it (row menu and the stand's Song menu alike, #1756), so one Delete removes the song. All songs removes every marked row behind one confirm. `purgeLocal` is LOCAL and ONE owner-fenced transaction over every id: the song row, every writer's draft, the id's WHOLE outbox queue (never a single op), the `remote:`/`delete:`/`opened:` rows; receipts stay, as `commitDeleted` keeps them. It queues nothing, freezes nothing and sends nothing; an id the account still holds, or a collection, comes back `held`, untouched. The loop's purge takes its ONE serialization slot (`inFlight`): it waits until no pass is running, and any `run()` meanwhile coalesces behind it, so this tab never sends a Save for an id it is deleting; then it publishes `libraryVersion` and `collectionsVersion`. Residual, accepted: another TAB mid-pass can still send a Save of a purged id — its `acknowledge` finds no operation and throws (deliberately), that pass shows the server failure once, and the song can reappear on the next download because the account then holds it; nothing is lost and the next pass is clean. A tab that has a purged song OPEN keeps writing drafts for it (rows with no record), and a later plain Save there is refused with `LocalRevisionError` ("saved elsewhere … duplicate it"), whose Duplicate keeps the music under a new id — the same posture another tab meets after `keepBoth`/`adoptRemoteVersion` move an id under it. `purgeConfirmed` drops the tab's in-memory drafts, opened-at entries and the guest-namespace recovery slot (`clearOwnRecovery`). Marked rows stay listed but leave the counts: All songs' header and filter, and the home's "All N songs" (`count - gone`, "All songs →" when that is 0, so a songbook of nothing else still reaches the remove button). Account only: the guest songbook never has a `deleted` candidate and its Delete is already local |
| Adopting a preserved remote update (#1310) | `lib/sync/repository.ts` (`adoptRemoteVersion`), `lib/account/sync-loop.ts`, `SyncSnapshot.candidates`, `app/account/adopt-remote.tsx` | The reconciliation half of `reconcile`'s `'candidate'`, and the mirror of Keep both: the account's version takes the ORIGINAL id, every writer's draft for it goes, and the candidate row goes with the divergence it described — ONE transaction, compare-and-swapped against the revision the CONFIRM STEP was opened against (frozen in `adoptRemoteOffer`, so a pass cannot re-base an open question; `'stale'` closes it and the banner already shows the newer one). Only `kind: 'version'` is adoptable (a stored candidate whose body is a text chart is never published since #1740: `remoteCandidates` steps over it). A `deleted` or `unsupported` candidate IS marked and banner-offered too (#1362 widened `SyncSnapshot.candidates` to publish every kind, not `version` alone) — the songbook row and the stand banner both read `kind`, and the sync chip stops claiming "Saved to your account" for a `deleted` one — but neither is ever the `candidate`/"Use the account's version" shape: `deleted` reuses `gone`'s "Keep mine as a new song" action instead (see the Keep-both row above), and `unsupported` offers no action at all. A document with ANYTHING in its outbox is refused (`'queued'`): what this discards must be work the musician never committed, and a queued Save has its own exit — refused on the next pass, then `keepBoth`. In `reconcile` the plan base is asked BEFORE `held`, and a body whose base no longer matches the saved record writes NOTHING and answers `'superseded'`. `adoptRemoteVersion` re-reads the record and drops a candidate whose revision the record already holds, for devices that stored one before the fix. The banner is non-modal because the one destructive choice opens its own confirm step with the export offered first; both it and that step branch on ONE dirtiness derivation, since a chart merely OPEN is held too and must not be told its "unsaved changes" will be discarded. The stand re-opens the adopted document through `open()` (so the runtime stops and reloads — Keep both's "nothing changes on the stand" is the opposite case, and it keeps the music). It does NOT sweep drafts again afterwards: this tab's earlier retention is ordered before the adoption by IndexedDB, and a later sweep could only destroy another tab's fresh work |

| Sign-out and session expiry (#1269, #1351) | `lib/account/sync-loop.ts` (`signOut`, `heldScope`, `heldOwner`), `lib/sync/repository.ts` (`clearAccount`), `app/account/sign-out.tsx`, `app/use-held-account.ts`, `heldAccountBanner` | Fence bumped BEFORE the logout request; local data cleared only once the server confirms the revocation. Expiry is the other thing: the outbox pauses, nothing local is removed — until the musician asks. **"Sign out on this device" (#1351)** is that ask, and it is the third caller of ONE clearing path (`switchAccount(null)` → revoke → `clearAccount`), differing only in how the server side is settled: #1269 awaits the logout, #1271 was deleted server-side, this one is already dead. `revoke` is a resolved `true`, so it sends NOTHING and works offline (rollout decision 9 S2 is about revoking, and there is nothing to revoke); no `refresh()` either. **The banner's condition is a STORAGE fact, not a session one** — `heldOwner()` (`meta.active`) non-null AND the session is `expired` *or* `guest`, never `unknown`. `signOut` refuses an UNNAMED claim when nothing is attached, and `heldScope` refuses a named one that is not what this device holds (`belongsToAnotherAccount`); the preflight, the library it exports from and its drafts resolve the same way, and so do `refreshSongs`/`computeAdoptCandidates`, which are gated on session facts while reading storage. A clear that fails AFTER the fence moved puts the owner back (retried once), so the offer — and the retry — stay reachable; if that restore also fails the sentence says "reload", never naming a control that is off screen. **The banner says three different things** (`heldAccountBanner` in `lib/account/messages.ts`): `expired` keeps #1269's "sign in again to keep syncing", `guest` states the fact with a plain "Sign in", and a deletion this tab ran offers NO sign-in at all — one sentence in all three states is a lie in two of them. No "Sync now" (nothing on this step can send), and the copy is scoped to the action: signing out here discards the queued Save, cancelling and signing back in still uploads it. Refusals render inside the modal, never behind it |
