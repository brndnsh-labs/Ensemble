Overview

Ensemble is a generative accompaniment engine. It coordinates a virtual band that adapts to your arrangement in real-time using two core systems:

  • Smart Grooves: Recalibrates rhythmic feel, swing, and micro-timing based on Genre. Features a Creativity toggle to enable generative, jam-session style variations.
  • The Conductor: A global signal controlling Band Intensity (energy, tone, voicing density) and Complexity (syncopation/harmony). Complexity can be adjusted in the Settings menu.

Arranger & Chords

Notation Syntax

The arranger supports three standard notation formats. You can mix them, but consistency is recommended.

Format Example Description
Absolute Cmaj7 Am7 Dm7 G7 Standard chord names. Good for specific songs.
Roman Imaj7 vi7 ii7 V7 Key-agnostic functional harmony. Best for transposing.
Nashville 1maj7 6-7 2-7 57 Studio standard numbers. Use - for minor.

🔣 Advanced Syntax

  • Slash Chords: Define a specific bass note using /. Works in all formats: C/E, I/3, 1/3.
  • Measures & Beats: Use | to separate measures. Chords are distributed evenly.
    C | F G | = 1 bar of C (4 beats), 1 bar of F then G (2 beats each).
  • Line Breaks: Each line in the text area is treated as a continuation of the progression.

Section Controls

Each section card in the arranger offers powerful overrides:

  • Key & Time Overrides: Set a specific Key or Time Signature for just that section (e.g., modulate to G Major for the Chorus).
  • Repeat (x): Loop a section multiple times (e.g., x4) to keep your arrangement compact.
  • Seamless Link (🔗): Connects a section to the previous one without a drum fill or crash. Essential for creating long, continuous phrases (e.g., an 8-bar verse split into two 4-bar cards).
  • Reorder (▴/▾): Move sections up or down in the arrangement.
  • Duplicate (⎘): Clone a section with all its settings.
  • Symbol Menu (⋮): Quickly insert complex jazz symbols (ø, °, maj7) via the kebab menu.

Global Tools

🔄 Intelligent Transposition

Located in the top toolbar:

  • Transpose (♭/♯): Shifts the entire song semitonally.
  • Relative Key (maj/min): Instantly swaps the song between its Relative Major and Minor keys (e.g., C Major ↔ A Minor). The engine intelligently rewrites the chords to preserve harmonic function.

✨ Advanced Song Generator (Dice Icon)

Replace your current arrangement with a complete, musically coherent song form in one click. Perfect for generating quick practice tracks or overcoming writer's block.

  • Flexible Formats: Choose between Standard Pop, Power Ballads, Blues Forms, or completely Random structures.
  • Smart Voice-Leading: Sections are generated with harmonic transitions that flow naturally from one to the next.
  • Key & Time Sync: Choose specific keys/meters or let the engine pick for you.

🧠 Intelligent Seeding

Have a great chord progression but don't know where to take it? Use Seeding to build a song around your ideas.

  • Contextual Building: Tell the generator to treat your current chords as the "Verse" or "Chorus."
  • Preservation: The engine will keep your exact chords for those sections while composing Intros, Bridges, and Outros that complement your riff.
  • Rapid Prototyping: Combine your custom motifs with procedural generation to finish songs in seconds.

Audio Workbench

A unified tool for analyzing audio files or live performances to build your arrangement.

🎼 Chord Detection

Analyzes polyphonic audio (e.g., a full song or piano track) to transcribe existing chord progressions into song sections.

🎤 Melody Harmonizer

Analyzes monophonic audio (e.g., a sung vocal or lead instrument) to generate matching backing chords. Features three harmonization strategies:

  • Consonant: Safe, diatonic choices that strictly follow the key.
  • Balanced: A mix of conventional harmony with colorful passing chords.
  • Complex: Prioritizes melody fit and color over key adherence.

Explainability: Tap any generated chord to see the theory reasoning behind the engine's choice.

👂 Live Listen

Real-time analysis using your microphone. Supports Chords mode (polyphonic) and Melody mode (monophonic).

  • Auto-Add: Automatically captures stable chords to the progression history as you play.
  • Import Sequence: Saves your performed progression directly to the arranger.

Instruments

Grooves (Drums)

16-step sequencer with auto-generated fills based on Band Intensity. Includes procedural percussion (Congas, Guiro, Shakers).

Creativity Mode: When enabled, the engine intelligently creates genre-authentic variations, ensuring the drum parts feel different during every session.

Smart Genres: Rock, Jazz, Funk, Disco, Hip Hop, Blues, Neo-Soul, Reggae, Acoustic, Bossa, Country, Metal, and Ska-Punk.

Manual Presets: Standard, Backbeat Only, Basic Rock, House, Trap, DnB, Afrobeat, Samba, Latin/Salsa, and more.

Bass

Generates lines based on style. Automatically handles slash chords (e.g., C/G) and register shifts.

Styles: Walking, Funk, Rocco (16ths), Disco (Octaves), Neo-Soul (Lag), Country (Root-Five), Metal (Gallop), Reggae (Dub), Bossa Nova, Rock (8th), Walking (Ska), Whole Note, Half Note, and Arpeggiated.

Soloist

Phrasing-aware improvisation. Uses "Loop-Back" logic to listen to the band's context.

Styles: Scalar, Country, Shreddy, Metal, Blues, Neo-Soul, Minimal, Bird (Jazz), Disco, and Ska Horns. (Smart mode adapts to Funk, Bossa, etc.)

Controls:
• Complexity: Adjust note density and syncopation independent of the band. (Located in the Soloist Settings (⋮) menu).
• Trade Mode: Automate call-and-response jamming. Choose Sections to alternate soloist state every section change, or Loops to alternate every song loop.
• Musical Entry: The soloist intelligently waits for the start of a measure (the "1") to enter, ensuring phrases always start on a strong beat.
• Status Indicator: The Soloist power button (⏻) indicates its current state:
   - Green: Soloist is active and playing.
   - Yellow: Soloist is enabled but waiting for its turn (Trading active).
   - Off: Soloist is disabled (Manual mode required to reactivate).

Devices: Runs, Double Stops, Enclosures, Quartal Harmony, Banjo Rolls, Slides, Country Bends, Chicken Pick, Grace Slides, Bird Flurry.

Harmony (Horns/Strings)

Generates background hooks and pads. Features Motif Memory for catchy, repeating seeds and Soloist Awareness (retreats to pads when the soloist is active).

Styles: Horns (Stabs), Strings (Pads), Organ (B3), Modern Synth (Plucks), Contrapuntal.

Visualizer

Real-time view using harmonic colors: Root (Blue), 3rd (Green), 5th (Orange), 7th+ (Violet).

MIDI & Export

Route performance to external DAWs (Logic, Ableton, etc.) or export standard MIDI files.

  • Enable: Settings > Enable Web MIDI Output.
  • Channels: Chords (1), Bass (2), Soloist (3), Harmony (4), Drums (10).
  • Configuration: Adjustable Latency Compensation and Velocity Sensitivity. Option to Mute Local Audio when MIDI is active.
  • Transport Sync: Ensemble sends MIDI Start/Stop messages to sync your DAW's playhead.
  • Expressive Automation: Soloist sends Pitch Bend (scoops) and Modulation (CC 1) based on tension. All tracks send Expression (CC 11) mapped to Band Intensity.
  • Panic (Reset): Double-clicking Stop or toggling MIDI sends a full reset (CC 121) and kills all active voices.
  • Monophony: Bass and Soloist tracks enforce strict monophony (clean re-attacks) to ensure reliability with hardware and vintage synth patches.
  • Export: Download a multi-track .mid file for offline editing.
