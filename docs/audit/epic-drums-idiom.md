# Epic 7: Drum Sound Design & Genre Idiom

## Why this epic exists

Three clusters in the drums audit:

1. **Sound-design wiring gaps**: groove engines write `soundName` strings that don't exist in `synth-drums.ts`. Crash accents never play a Crash (they route to "Open"); Disco "Octave Cowbells" hit a missing voice; Jazz has no Brush voice. The Crash misroute affects EVERY genre that wants a section-transition splash.

2. **Entropy phase corrupts quiet sections**: `groove-engine.ts:233-272`'s random sprinkle runs at the same probability regardless of genre intent. Reggae One Drop holes get filled by phantom snares; Jazz intentional ride emptiness is contaminated; the `firstIterationSuppression` flag flips backwards when `totalSteps` is unset.

3. **Fill vocabulary is one-dimensional**: 9 of 15 genres never play a tom. Metal blast beat is co-articulation rather than alternation. Hip-hop "trap rolls" don't actually roll. Section boundaries outside Rock/Blues/Disco/Ska-Punk get no crash.

Plus a sprinkling of genre-specific motif fixes (Country train beat continuity, Latin Snare body, Ska-Punk D-beat pattern, Acoustic motif 0 is mislabeled).

## Stories

### S1. Crash routing: wire accent-catch + section-boundary to the real Crash voice
`groove-engine.ts:202-210` and `:146-160` write `soundName = 'Open'` where they mean Crash. `synth-drums.ts:285/937` has a real Crash voice that's never reached. Route `crash-catch` and post-turnaround downbeat to a Crash instrument lane. Tie into `drum-seeder.ts:265-274`'s "Crash Contract" so `pendingCrash` actually emits a Crash event on the next downbeat after a fill.

**Acceptance:** section transitions in Funk/Jazz/Country/Acoustic/Hip-Hop/Neo-Soul/Reggae produce an audible Crash splash. New critique test asserts crash-event count rises with section transitions.
**Effort:** ~4h. **Model:** sonnet (route soundName + pendingCrash consumer). **Reviewer:** music-theory-reviewer. **Source:** `drums.md` P0 #1, P1 #14.
**Status:** Shipped 2026-05-17 — `groove-engine.ts` turnaround block moved post-strategy (genre `applyOverrides` was wiping the route); both turnaround + crash-catch accent blocks now emit `soundName = 'Crash'` scoped to the Open lane only (the `blues.ts:59` single-lane pattern — previous review caught that firing on HiHat+Open lanes produced two stacked Crash drumHits per boundary, with the second voice's `lastCrashGain` ramp-down choking the first). New critique test `tests/standards/crash-routing-critique.test.ts` (per-genre boundary, crash-catch accent, mid-section steady-state, 5/5 reliability runs at 36/36).

### S2. Add Cowbell + Brush voices (or fall back) and audit `soundName` mismatches
Disco motif 3 writes `'CowbellHigh'` / `'CowbellLow'` — no handler. Jazz at low intensity wants brushes — no Brush voice exists. Either add the voices to `synth-drums.ts` (Cowbell: resonant ~800 Hz + bandpass click; Brush: long noise sweep with bandpass automation), or fall back to nearest-timbre (`AgogoHigh`/`AgogoLow` for cowbell as a stopgap). Also add a `KNOWN_SOUND_NAMES` runtime check that warns when a groove writes an unknown name.

**Acceptance:** Disco Octave Percussion is audible. Jazz ballad has brush texture below intensity 0.35. Future `soundName` typos surface as console warnings.
**Effort:** ~6h. **Model:** opus (sound design — see `feedback-synth-audio-graph` for cymbal/noise voice gotchas). **Reviewer:** music-theory-reviewer. **Source:** `drums.md` P0 #3, P1 #7.
**Status:** Shipped 2026-05-18 — full synthesis for both voices (no fallback). Cowbell uses TR-808 canonical dual-fundamental at 540 Hz + 800 Hz (the 540:800 ≈ 1.48 ratio IS the inharmonic clang); CowbellHigh emphasizes 800 with 540 trimmed, CowbellLow inverts the trim — preserves octave gesture via emphasis rather than pitch. Initial implementation used a sine partial at 1.55× per fundamental and reviewer caught that this made CowbellHigh read as a tuned bell — the dual-fundamental approach is closer to the LP56 reference. Decay tightened to 0.04s (envelope <2% by t=0.18) to avoid click on motif-3 eighth-note tail. Brush voice synthesizes a looped pink-noise sweep through a bandpass (2.4kHz → 1.4kHz over 0.18s) with 35ms slow attack — the "swish" character; jazz routes Snare→Brush when `intensity < 0.35 && bpm < 130`; velocity captured pre-Sidestick-trim so Brush restore is explicit rather than `/= 0.8` math. `KNOWN_SOUND_NAMES` set + `maybeWarnUnknownSound` helper warns once per unknown name per session (exempts dynamic Tom/Conga/Bongo/Agogo/Cowbell family substring matches). New critique test `tests/standards/cowbell-brush-voices-critique.test.ts` — 7 tests, 5/5 reliability runs: Disco motif-3 132 cowbell hits over 64 bars at intensity 0.95 + 0 hits at 0.3; Jazz brush share >0.95 of snare lane at intensity 0.25/BPM 90 + 0 brush hits at BPM 180 or intensity 0.5. Six P2 follow-ups filed (Brush pan/envelope/comment polish, MIDI export gap for CowbellHigh/Low, KNOWN_SOUND_NAMES exemption tightening).

### S4. Tom vocabulary across 9 genres
`fills.ts FILL_TEMPLATES` has toms only in Rock + Bossa. Add genre-appropriate tom templates for Funk, Country, Blues, Neo-Soul, Hip-Hop, Disco (and Acoustic where appropriate). Add a Toms lane to those grooves' fill-firing paths.

**Acceptance:** fills across the listed genres include tom hits. Audible: section transitions feel like real drum fills, not snare rolls. New cross-genre fill-content test.
**Effort:** ~5h. **Model:** opus (per-genre tom vocabulary is a musical design call). **Reviewer:** music-theory-reviewer. **Source:** `drums.md` P1 #5.
**Status:** Shipped 2026-05-18 — added tom-bearing templates to `FILL_TEMPLATES` for Funk (medium+high), Country (new entry with pure-snare train-beat + H→M→L→Kick tom roll + 16th train-fill), Blues (medium+high), Disco (medium+high), Acoustic (medium+high), plus new `'Hip Hop'` and `'Neo-Soul'` entries — previously these genres fell back to Rock fills (Bonham-style tom rolls inside a country/hip-hop/neo-soul track). Each template carries a `// why:` comment naming the idiomatic reference (Stubblefield funk tom-syncopation on "e and &", Nashville session-drummer train-fill, Fred Below blues tom-down, Earth Wind & Fire disco tumble, trap producer tom drop, Questlove Neo-Soul tom-down, folk-acoustic ghost tom). No groove-file wiring needed: the fill consumer at `tick-logic.ts:284-298` iterates any soundName in the template and falls back to a virtual instrument if not registered, so new tom-bearing templates fire immediately. New critique test `tests/standards/tom-vocabulary-critique.test.ts` — 30 tests across 4 describe blocks (registry contract, 120-seed medium sweep, 120-seed high sweep, tom-voice diversity for all 7 genres including Hip Hop), 5/5 reliability runs. Reviewer caught six P1s — all patched inline: sweep threshold raised 15→30 (catches 2-template silent regressions to snare-only); added pure-snare Country medium template (was 100% tom-bearing, now ~67%); Funk WHY comment corrected ("e and &" matches steps 13/14, not "e and a"); Blues WHY rewritten to drop the false triplet-grid claim + re-credited to Fred Below (drummer); Neo-Soul "≤0.95" comment dropped (the displaced final hit at 1.0 is the gesture); Hip Hop added to diversity test (its high template uses Low Tom + Mid Tom — passes on its own merit). Two pre-existing P2s filed to FOLLOWUPS.md (Reggae+Ska-Punk tom templates dropped from S4 scope; `KNOWN_SOUND_NAMES` inert no-space tom variants).

### S5. Metal blast beat alternation + China
`grooves/metal.ts:62-156` Motif 4 stacks snare-on-8ths against kick-on-8ths (co-articulation). Real blast beats ALTERNATE: snare on `isBeatStart`, kick on `isOffbeat`. Add a `China` cymbal lane for genre tell. Critique test asserts alternation pattern, not unison.

**Acceptance:** Metal motif 4 produces the buzzing blast-beat texture. China cymbal audible at section accents.
**Effort:** ~3h. **Model:** opus (musical correctness of the blast-beat alternation pattern). **Reviewer:** music-theory-reviewer. **Source:** `drums.md` P1 #6.
**Status:** Shipped 2026-05-18 — Metal motif 4 split from motif 3: kick fires only on `isOffbeat` (steps 2, 6, 10, 14), snare only on `isBeatStart` (steps 0, 4, 8, 12) — the canonical extreme-metal blast alternation (validated by reviewer against published pedagogy). New `China` cymbal voice in `synth-drums.ts` — its own entry in `CymbalName`, `CYMBAL_BUFFER_PROFILES` (baseFreq 1100, duration 5.0s, partialDecay 1.6 = ~2× faster choke than Crash's 0.8 — implementer correctly inverted the audit-doc's `~0.45` suggestion which would have made China decay slower; reviewer confirmed the math), `CYMBAL_RUNTIME_PROFILES` (bandpassBase 4200, decayBase 0.95s, stopTime 2.4s — bark-not-wash), `RIGHT_PANNED_INSTRUMENTS`, `KNOWN_SOUND_NAMES`; Crash dispatch branch widened to handle `name === 'China'` with parameterized buffer key. Reviewer caught a P0: the metal.ts accent block ran for HiHat + Open + Crash inst lanes, so each downbeat at intensity > 0.8 fired three China voices that choked each other via `groove.lastCrashGain` — exact regression class the S1 commit at `groove-engine.ts:226-237` fixed for Crash. Patched inline: scoped the accent to `inst.name === 'Open'` only (matches the post-turnaround + crash-catch convention). Plus a P1 test gap: original China test sampled only the Crash lane, so it would have passed identically with the triple-stack — tightened to assert China on Open AND non-China on HiHat/Crash on the same downbeat. Existing blast-beat critique test was codifying the unison bug (counted `snareKickLocks` and asserted high) — replaced with alternation assertions: `snareKickLocksOnBlast === 0`, `snareOnOffbeatOnBlast === 0`, `kickOnDownbeatOnBlast === 0` over 128 bars. Shred test thresholds re-anchored to the new alternation pattern (Shred passthrough-aliases Metal). 5/5 reliability runs at 8/8 tests; full standards sweep 535/535. Two P2s filed to `FOLLOWUPS.md` § E: China `volumeScale` recalibration after the triple-stack fix (was tuned defensively for the buggy case); post-turnaround `'Crash'` → `'China'` on Metal sections via a genre `accentCymbal` config field.

### S6. Hip-hop trap hat rolls + funk displaced backbeat
Two related fixes:
- `grooves/hiphop.ts:106-180` motif 2 (labeled "Trap Skitter") fires 16ths but never produces the genre-defining 32nd/triplet roll. When `activeMotif >= 2 && phraseSeed > 0.7`, schedule a 4-6 hit burst at triplet subdivision on one beat per bar.
- `grooves/funk.ts:168-181` motif 2 uses 50/50 coin flips for "displaced backbeat" — that's scatter, not displacement. Use `phraseSeed` to pick a displacement amount once per phrase (snare lands at `[5, 13]` for an entire phrase, returns next phrase).

**Acceptance:** hip-hop trap rolls audible; funk displacement is structural, not stochastic. Add per-motif tests to each file.
**Effort:** ~4h. **Model:** opus (trap-roll burst design + funk displacement structure both need musical judgment). **Reviewer:** music-theory-reviewer. **Source:** `drums.md` P1 #8, #9.
**Status:** Shipped 2026-05-18 — Hip-hop trap roll: at `activeMotif >= 2` and `rollPhraseSeed > 0.7` (distinct salt 11), all 4 16ths of one chosen beat fire HiHat with strict velocity ramp 0.55 → 0.6 → 0.7 → 0.85 and forward `instTimeOffset` skew +0.001…+0.0025 ("rush" toward next downbeat). Beat selection weighted toward beat 3 (50% beat 3 / 25% beat 2 / 25% beat 1) — beat 3 leads INTO next bar's downbeat, the strongest musical target. Beat 0 excluded (kick-on-One stays clean). Skitter logic short-circuited on burst beat. Funk motif-2 displacement: `getPhraseSeed(sectionSeed, barIndex, 2, 17)` locks each 2-bar phrase to one of three buckets — 40% normal {4,12} / 35% +1 {5,13} / 25% +2 {6,14} — both backbeats land at the SAME displaced offset for the whole phrase (structural, not scattered). Voice resolution block unchanged. Reviewer caught a P0: original burst clauses left `boomBapOpen`/`trapOpen` Open-release firing on the burst beat at vel 1.02, punching through the ramp peak and silently passing the burst test (Open-converted slots were excluded from the burst count rather than flagged — smell e). Patched: gated `(boomBapOpen || trapOpen) && !trapRollHere`; added positive guard in test asserting `openHitsOnBurstBeat === 0`. Beat-selection distribution mislabeled "thirds" in original (was actually 50/25/25 across beats 1/2/3); re-bucketed to beat-3-weighted with musical justification. Funk fixture got `beatIndex` (engine reads it; fixture omission would have silenced the displacement branch — smell e). New `hiphop-drummer-critique` burst test + new `funk-drummer-critique` structural-displacement test + `funk-groove-integrity` "Cold Sweat" test rewritten from old stochastic contract to new per-phrase contract. 5/5 reliability on touched files; full `tests/standards/` 537/537. Two P2s filed to `FOLLOWUPS.md` § E (funk +2 frequency tuning + test floor tightening after 20-run sample).
