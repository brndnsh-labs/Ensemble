# Musical Audit (Archived Snapshot)

Snapshot of the **2026-05-16 → 2026-05-19 musical audit cycle**. Chapter closed at 50/50 stories shipped across 8 epics.

Live engine-pattern recipes (5 smells, coordination patterns, loop-awareness, final-stage multiplier discipline, dual-gate activation, seeded determinism) extracted to **`docs/guides/musical-engine-patterns.md`** — that's where to look for ongoing engine-work reference.

Post-audit follow-ups (deferred items surfaced during the cycle) live in **`docs/audit/FOLLOWUPS.md`**. The per-area findings docs (`docs/audit/{soloist,bass,chords,drums,harmony-coordination,form-arranger}.md`) remain as historical inputs to the cycle.

## Cycle summary

- **Trigger:** 2026-05-16 parallel music-theory-reviewer pass — six reviewers, 95 findings across six area files.
- **Synthesis:** findings curated into 8 epics × 49 session-sized stories (50 after Epic 2 listening-test surfaced S8).
- **Phased rollout:** Phase 1 (Epic 1 + Epic 3 S1-S2, sequential opus, foundation) → Phase 2 (sonnet fan-out, ~3-5 agents in parallel on disjoint files) → Phase 3 (opus per-story, parallel).
- **Reviewer discipline:** every story reviewed on the uncommitted diff by `music-theory-reviewer` before commit; `state-discipline-reviewer` and `worker-contract-reviewer` engaged when slices changed.
- **P0 rate:** ~60% of stories surfaced at least one P0 reviewer finding ("audit doc named the right place but the recipe didn't survive the gate set"). Budget for premise-fix patches became part of the cycle rhythm.
- **Determinism:** seeded mulberry32 (`scrambleHash`) replaced bare `Math.random()` across bass, drums, harmonies, accompaniment.
- **Test discipline:** 30-run reliability loop (`for i in $(seq 1 30); do npx vitest run ... | grep FAIL; done`) before locking thresholds. ~80 critique tests in `tests/standards/` at cycle close, up from ~50 at start.
