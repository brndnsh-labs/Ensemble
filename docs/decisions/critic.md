## a take read as a transcription (2026-11-20, #2231)

`ensemble read` prints a take bar by bar, each seat as a transcriber would write it down, for a reader who knows music and cannot hear (the `ear-reviewer` agent first). The calls the issue left open:

- **Bars are numbered from the first bar of music**, so bar 13 is the same bar with or without a count-in. The count-in is left out unless something other than sticks starts in it, and a tail only when a note starts in it.
- **Where a note is, in the bar's own counting, one token a place.** By the quarter: `3`, `3e`, `3&`, `3a`, triplet eighths `3t` and `3l`. By the dotted pulse (6/8, 12/8): `2`, `2la`, `2li`, a sixteenth after any of them `2+`, `2la+`, `2li+`. Off that grid, the pulse and the fraction of it: `3+5/12`. A pulse of another length (the long last pulse of 7/8) is always a fraction. It is the straight grid; `--timing` adds `@+N`, the played offset in ticks.
- **Lengths are as sounded**, in quarters, and in eighths in compound time; velocity as played (`v72`).
- **Roles are from the chord's root and named by the symbol where it has the tone** (`b3`, `#9`, `13`, `bb7` in a diminished seventh). A note the symbol lacks gets the plain interval with `*` (`4*`, `13*`), and never `out`: `theory/chord.ts` knows the symbol's tones, not which tensions a style allows, so "out" cannot be decided there.
- **A push is read against the chord coming**: a note or strike within an eighth before a change, more of whose pitches belong to the coming chord than the one under it, is read against the coming one and marked `^`. Otherwise every pushed comp chord would read as a wrong voicing.
- **A strike is the chord seat's notes on one grid tick**: a strum's strokes and a rolled chord are nudged off the grid (`nudge`) and stay one strike. Its length is the longest note's and its level the loudest's. Every pitch shows its role (the guide tones are what Q-01 asks), and a strike with the same notes as the one before it in the bar is `=`, which keeps a strummed bar to one line. `x` is a muted scratch, `pick` a plucked string.
- **Moves are named by their shape as `players/solo` writes them**: two glide points from under the note up to it is a `bendN` on an instrument that bends and a `scoopN` on one that scoops; from the note down after its length is `fallN`; stepped onto the note a fret at a time is `slide-N` (from under) or `slide+N` (from over); a vibrato is `shake`; a slur is `hammer` up or `pull` down from the note before. Anything else is `move`.
- **Drums by voice, ghost and accent by stated rules**: a ghost is the ghost-snare voice (the engine's own line, a written level under 50), in parentheses; an accent `>` is a stroke 15% over the median of its voice in the bar, among three or more. A relative accent, because velocities follow the bar's energy.
- **A soloist's phrase ends at a rest of a pulse or more** after the note's sounded end (a quarter, a dotted quarter in compound time): ` | §15` in the line. Phrases are numbered across the whole take, so `--bars` does not renumber them.
- **Pitches are spelled by the key's lean** (`spellPitchClass`), as the issue said: a b7 of C7 in G reads `A#`. Spelling by the chord would read better; it would be a second speller beside the engine's.
- `render`'s take flags moved into `take-output.ts` (`TAKE_FLAGS`, `takeOf`), shared by `render` and `read`; `render` writes byte-identical MIDI and the same messages.

## a change is sorted by measurement before his ear is asked (2026-11-20, #2232)

Every change that could be heard waited on Brandon's ear. Most stay inside what he has already accepted, and the ear cases (#2230) say what that is: for each measure, the range of its mean over the bands he has passed since that seat was last put right.

- **`pnpm score` sorts the change in hand.** Each style's report ends "against what he has passed by ear": inside what was passed, or the measures the change took outside, each with its value, the range and how many passes made it. The ranges and their reading moved from `ears.ts` into `packages/cli/src/passed.ts`, shared by both commands; `pnpm ears` prints what it printed.
- **Only what the change moved counts against it.** The change is what moved since the branch left `main` (`--since <ref>` names another commit), read from the scorecard in git and not the one on disk, so accepting a baseline does not make its change look like no change. A measure already outside his range and no further out is named and left: today that is one to three measures in funk, rock, disco, reggae and acoustic, mostly `chords.overlap`, which drifted out with the busier soloist he then passed. A measure further out by more than the scorecard's "no movement" (0.005) counts.
- **A measure no pass covers** (jazz's and reggae's backbeat levels, neo-soul's drums since the late four) counts when it moves at all. **A measure the change brought** is outside by definition, and so is **one it took away**.
- **Toward a band he refused there is no slack.** Tried on a planted fault before it was trusted: the blues soloist's `busier` set to nothing, which undoes about half of what he asked for on 2026-11-17, moved 26 measures and read "inside", because a range's slack (a quarter of the spread) is wider than the step he heard. With it the planted fault is named on `solo.notes-per-second-first` and `solo.rises`. `pnpm ears` does not use the fences: its count of faults the passed spans would catch would be true by construction.
- **The rule, in the overlays**: goldens unmoved, nothing to hear; a new kind of sound, always his; otherwise inside in every style merges and closes unheard, outside in any style gets the page and `status:needs-ear` with the measures that left named first. An inside change does not count toward a seat's two unheard.
- **The sample is by issue number**: an inside change whose issue number divides by five goes to his ear anyway. Nothing to keep count of, and nobody picks.
- **A verdict has to come back as a case** or the ranges never move: `/unblock` now says to commit his words, then add the case naming that commit. This was missing.
- **Only the accepted run is sorted**: the style's whole library on 200 takes. A run on named charts, another count of seeds or another baseline prints no sort, since its numbers are not the ones he passed. CI's clone has no history, so there it says "not read".
- **Tried on the last three musical changes he judged.** The busier soloist (`f2cea737`): outside in all eight styles (`solo.notes-per-second-first` in seven, `solo.empty-bars` in six), so to his ear; he heard it and passed it. The sort agreed with what happened twice, and the third shows what it cannot see.
