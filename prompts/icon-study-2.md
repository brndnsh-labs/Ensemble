## The owner's words

Of the nine candidates, 2026-11-18: "i liked b the best, but i don't think we're done with it yet. how can we make it look more musical?"

B is "The wall" (`out/icon-study/icons/b-wall.svg`): five tiles of style ink set edge to edge, each with a short ink rule, the biggest carrying an E. He chose the wall: the tiles, the spot inks meeting edge to edge, the flatness. What it lacks is anything that says music: an E and four dashes could be a notes app or a news app.

## What to make

**Eight variations on B**, each keeping what he chose (tiles of the styles' spot inks, edge to edge, full bleed, flat, ink on colour, the app's own inks) and each a different answer to "more musical". The music comes from what the tiles are or carry, in the chart's and the band's own terms, not from clip art (still no eighth-note pair, no treble clef, no instrument silhouette). Directions to draw from; use the ones that hold up, add your own, and do not make two of one idea:

- **The tiles are bars.** A line of chart is a row of bars: tiles as bars of a chart, with what a bar carries in the app's own chart (a chord symbol in the chart's serif, slashes, a repeat sign, a bar line between tiles in place of a seam).
- **One tile is being played.** The app marks the bar being played with the yellow marker: one tile in the marker's yellow, or the marker's stroke over one tile, among the spot inks.
- **The tiles are time.** Their widths as lengths of notes or beats (a long, two shorts, a long: a rhythm you could clap), the rules in them as held notes, the wall read left to right as a bar.
- **The tiles are the band.** Four tiles for four players, each carrying the plainest mark of what it does in ink (hits, a walking line, a block of chord, a single line that rises and falls), abstract as the rules are now.
- **The E is made of music.** The E kept, but built of what a chart is built of (slashes, bar lines, a repeat sign's dots and lines), or sitting where a chord symbol sits (E7, set as the chart sets a chord).
- **What the rules become.** The four ink rules are B's weakest part: as five of them they are a staff; staggered they are a melody's contour or a piano roll; thick and thin with two dots they are a repeat sign.

Keep the E in at least half of them and leave it out of at least two, so he can see whether the letter matters. Keep B itself on the page, unchanged, marked as the one he chose from.

Everything the first round asked of an icon is still asked: the iPhone's home screen at 180 px on light and dark wallpapers, Android's circle (B lost its outer tiles and its E sat off-centre there: these should not), a tab at 32 and 16 px (a simpler favicon of the same idea where needed), 512 px. Eight inks at once is noise at 180 px: B used five; say how many each uses and why.
