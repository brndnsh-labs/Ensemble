Blues, jazz, funk, rock and disco exist; the first four were auditioned by the owner (a bassist and guitarist) and passed. Neo-soul is the third of five more. Rock and disco were built the same way you are about to build neo-soul, from `prompts/style-rock.md` and `prompts/style-disco.md`; their sections in `DECISIONS.md` ("rock", "disco", and the "checked and merged" section after each, which says what the main session changed) are the nearest thing to a worked example.

## What to build

1. **`packages/styles/src/neosoul/`** (style id `neosoul`, name "Neo-soul"): `index.ts`, `drums.ts`, `bass.ts`, `chords.ts`, `solo.ts`, `critic.ts`, and one line in `packages/styles/src/index.ts`. Written from the neo-soul notes. Where a note is marked solid, follow it; where it says unclear, decide and log. The chord seat is the Rhodes (ST-neosoul-19 to 25); the guitar book (ST-neosoul-26 on) is not part of this build.
2. **Sound**: packs `drum-kit`, `electric-bass` (it has a `dead` voice) and `electric-piano` (new: a Rhodes, cut from full-length takes at five dynamics; it has never been in a band, so its level is unmeasured), a mix in the style's `sound` in the manner of the others, General MIDI programs for the export (electric piano 1 is program 4, counting from 0). The notes' lead is a clean electric guitar (ST-neosoul-43). The `clean-guitar` pack exists but is not a soloist's instrument yet: add it to `SOLO_INSTRUMENTS` (`packages/engine/src/players/solo/instruments.ts`) beside the overdriven guitar, with the same register and the same hand (bend and shake) unless the notes say otherwise, make it this style's default, and check that the app's soloist control and `pnpm levels` pick it up by themselves. The soloist's bend and shake habits are style data (`SoloStyle.habits.bend`, `.shake`): give neo-soul the numbers ST-neosoul-33 has. A slide into a note is not in the instrument model and is not played. Do not invent an instrument: no organ, no strings.
3. **Library**: five or six charts in `library/neosoul-*.chart`, in the manner of the others (look at their headers and how plain they are): the forms a neo-soul rhythm section is asked for most (for example a two-chord vamp of ninths, a four-chord loop with a borrowed chord, a minor loop with a dominant that turns it round, a slow two-five-one with extensions, and one song with an intro, verses, a chorus, a bridge and an outro). Give the song chart an arrangement block that fits it (`docs/CHART_FORMAT.md` section 11). Generic progressions only: no chart may be a named song.
4. **The critic and the scorecard**: `neosoul/critic.ts` with weights and targets from the notes' measured numbers (ST-neosoul-12, ST-neosoul-15, ST-neosoul-18, ST-neosoul-20 and what the other styles' critics show is needed), then `pnpm -s ensemble score --style neosoul --update` to accept `scorecard/neosoul.json` once the numbers are where the notes say they should be. Say in your report what each target is and where the style sits against it.
5. **A golden take or two** for neo-soul, the way `test/goldens.ts` holds the others, with the line in `DECISIONS.md` that the rules ask for.

## The rules of this build

- **Styles are data.** If neo-soul cannot be written in the data as it stands, the engine is missing a concept. A small general concept you may add, saying in your report why it is general and not a special case for neo-soul. If it is not small (a new way of playing for a seat, a change to the planner), **stop and report what is missing** instead of building it: that is a design call for the main session. Things the notes ask for that may or may not exist yet, to find out early: swing on the sixteenth at a set share of the pair (ST-neosoul-02), a lean of 25 to 32 ms for each pitched seat with the drums on the grid (ST-neosoul-03), a hat part played at a share of its written level (ST-neosoul-09), a backbeat moved a sixteenth late on the second bar of a pair, which makes the loop two bars long (ST-neosoul-08; funk's beat is two bars long already), a bass hammer-on (ST-neosoul-13, 14), the two-handed spread with a left-hand shell and fourths above it (ST-neosoul-19), a comp that closes up under a live phrase and opens in the gaps (ST-neosoul-21, 24), a soft ending with no set-up fill (ST-neosoul-40), and rules for other meters (ST-neosoul-05).
- **The other five styles must not change.** Their goldens and scorecards must hold exactly. If an engine change of yours moves them, it was not general.
- **No new properties** unless neo-soul breaks a rule a musician would name and no existing property can see it; then one, stated as a rule about music, and say so. **Do not loosen an existing property to let the new style through** without saying exactly what you loosened and why in your report: the last build excused more than it needed to, and one of its "bugs in another style" was a test that said more than the engine's rule. If a property seems wrong for this style, say what the rule should be. The suite's budget grows with the styles: 14 seconds with five, 16 with six (`scripts/budget.ts`, `docs/TESTING.md`), and it stands at about 11.7 with five, each style in a process of its own, side by side, on 8 cores. Measure `pnpm check` with neo-soul added and report the number. If it goes over 16, do not trim anything: report it.
- **You cannot hear.** Never say it sounds good. Print takes and read them (`pnpm -s ensemble render library/neosoul-….chart --style neosoul --seed 1 --out x.mid`, and whatever the other builds used to read a part as text), measure them against the notes, and report the measurements. The owner will listen on the app.
- **Levels.** `pnpm levels neosoul` renders each seat alone and reads its loudness (it needs Google Chrome and ffmpeg; it serves from the repo, which works under `.work/` now). Set the style's mix so the whole band reads near the other styles (funk, rock and disco read -17.0 LUFS with everyone playing, blues and jazz -18.5; aim for -17.5 to -18.5 here, a quiet style) and the soloist about a decibel under the band, and report the numbers. ST-neosoul-44's make-up gain belongs to the old engine's mixer; use it as a hint, not a number.
- The app picks the style up by itself (the Style control and the library's shelves read `STYLES`). `pnpm smoke` must still pass.

## How to check your work

- `pnpm check`, `pnpm test:full`, `pnpm score` and `pnpm smoke` must pass. If the suite goes over budget while you are running other things, that is contention: rerun it alone before believing it.
- Read at least a low, a mid and a high pass of each seat on two charts and compare them with the notes' grids, bar by bar, before you call a seat done.
- Never `pkill -f`. Never wrap node in `timeout`. In zsh, do not name a loop variable `path`. Stop any server you start by its port (`fuser -k <port>/tcp`), and use ports 8770 and 8771 only.
