The electric bass: touch and tone

The same bass line six ways in each style, the first as the band plays it now. A softer touch plays the bass's gentler recordings at the level the line is written at: the styles play its two hardest sets of recordings almost all the time, and those are the bright ones. The tone is the bass's own tone control turned down. The last column is the bass alone, measured: its loudness, and how much of its energy lies above 800 Hz (lower is darker).
